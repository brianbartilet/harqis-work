The person behind the activity

How Facets work

You have more than one story to tell.

Programmer, parent, collector, mentor—each facet gives a part of your life its own context. Help a shared conversation understand the experience behind your activity.

01 · Name it

Start with a part of your life

Create a facet and choose a name and color. Try Programmer and tester, Parent, or Collector. You can edit it as your experience grows.

02 · Tell your story

Give your Soul some substance

Use the Soul field for experience, values, milestones, and things that matter to you. Markdown headings create sections you can choose separately when sharing.

Click the rendered text to edit. Add supporting files in Attachments, then review the extracted text.

03 · Connect

Let activity add context

Link relevant Spaces and related entities. Spaces help suggest the facet when building a share and supply activity for analysis.

For example, connect your programmer facet to a Work Space and add your career history to enrich an interview share.

04 · Review and share

Choose what a recipient learns

In a share recipe’s Personal context, select the Soul sections, attachment excerpts, or approved observations you want to include.

Original file downloads are a separate choice. Selected context is frozen when published; later facet edits do not automatically update existing shares.

Your story stays under your control

Creating a facet, linking a Space, or approving an observation does not publish anything. Choose personal evidence explicitly when preparing a share.

Two agents, two different jobs

For you · Analysis agent

Notice patterns in your activity

Analyze now and scheduled weekly analysis check active facets with linked Spaces. The agent uses your facet’s name and Soul alongside new or changed evidence from recent entries and linked actions and ideas.

It adds evidence-linked suggestions to the private Agent journal. Review them as inferences: approve, reject, or reset each observation. Approval makes it available for selection in a recipe; it does not share it or rewrite your Soul.

Analysis history shows what each run checked, what it found, and any deferred work or errors. Runs also create an HFL receipt with source persona-audit.

For your recipient · Share chat agent

Answer from the context you selected

When chat is available on a published share page, the agent can use the included Facet excerpts alongside that share’s activity. It is instructed to ground answers in this evidence and link claims to citations.

It does not browse your entire Facet library, private journal, or unselected attachments. If the supplied evidence cannot answer a question, it is instructed to say so. The conversation is read-only: it cannot edit your Facet or approve journal notes.

From your Facet to a shared conversation

  1. Prepare your Facet. Save your Soul and attachments, review extracted text, and approve any journal observations you want to make available.
  2. Build a share recipe. In Personal context · Facets and Soul, choose individual sections, attachment excerpts, and approved observations. Linked Spaces suggest relevant context; nothing is checked automatically.
  3. Shape the conversation. Optionally add the target company or role, audience, tone, job description, or icebreaker guidance. These guide answers; a job requirement is not evidence that you have that qualification. Guidance may appear in answers, so include only what you want the recipient to learn.
  4. Preview and publish. Review your selected excerpts in Agent → Personal context and use Test agent to check its answers. Original-file downloads require a separate choice. Publish the share with its access controls when you are happy with the context.
  5. The recipient reads and asks. The share page displays the included excerpts under About the sharer. Its chat can connect that background with shared activity—for example, career experience with evidence of a recent testing project.
Selected excerpts are visible to the recipient

Personal context is shared evidence, not a hidden instruction for the agent. Leave out anything you would not want shown directly on the share page.

What happens when your story changes?

Recipes retain the personal excerpts and revisions you selected. Editing a Facet or approving a new observation does not silently replace them—even when activity in a share is live. Review and select the updated context in the recipe, then update or generate the intended share explicitly.

History in the editor shows saved Facet revisions. Archive a facet when it is no longer active; use Show archived to restore it. Archiving does not revoke published shares. Manage their access from Sharing.
