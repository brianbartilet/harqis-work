How Spaces work

A Space is a named set of activity filters and linked objects. Create one for work, family, learning, or a project. It is private until you deliberately publish a share.

  1. Choose what belongs. Set sources, search text or #tags, and dates here. On Activity Corpus, Graph, or Kanban, use Manage Spaces to bring current filters here for review and saving.
  2. Connect objects. Assign actions, ideas, and boards to Spaces in their existing activity and board editors. Those links are separate from the saved entry filters.
  3. Focus or combine. Select one or more Spaces in the Space dropdown on Activity Corpus, Graph, or Kanban. Combined Spaces include matching activity without duplicates. Additional filters narrow the result.
  4. Follow a rhythm. Add weekly time blocks, then enable Dynamic mode in the dropdown. Overlapping blocks combine Spaces; gaps show all activity. Visualize displays the schedule. Manual selection turns Dynamic mode off.
  5. Share deliberately. Sharing preview explains the selected context. Generate share recipe opens a draft to review before publishing. Linking a Facet suggests personal context; it never publishes it automatically.

Schedules use your Profile timezone. An end before the start continues into the next day. Rolling dates move with today; fixed dates stay fixed. Removing a Space removes its saved filters and schedule, not your entries or other objects. Review linked Facets and object assignments afterward. Existing published shares follow their own frozen or live settings.
