A little orientation

How Spaces work

A place for each part of your life.

Bring activity together around work, family, learning, or a project. A Space combines saved entry filters with objects you link to it.

01 · Gather

Choose what belongs

Save sources, search text or #tags, and dates. Use Manage Spaces in Corpus, Graph, or Kanban to bring filters here, then choose a destination and save.

For several selected Spaces, only the additional filters carry over—not their combined rules. Review before saving.

02 · Connect

Add your context

Link actions, ideas, and boards in their existing editors. These assignments are separate from entry filters. Link a Facet to suggest relevant personal context when sharing.

03 · Focus

Follow your rhythm

Choose one or more Spaces in Focus on. Matching activity combines without duplicates; additional filters narrow it.

Add weekly time blocks here, then enable Dynamic mode. Overlaps combine Spaces; gaps show all activity. Visualize displays your schedule. Manual selection turns Dynamic mode off.

04 · Share

Decide what others see

Use Sharing preview to review the context. Generate share recipe opens a draft for review before publishing. Search text does not transfer to a recipe.

Choose the Facet excerpts each recipient may receive. Published shares follow their own frozen or live settings.

One Space, three ways to explore

Select a Space in Focus on to carry the same context across Activity Corpus, Kanban, and Graph. Your Space selection, saved Space filters, and Dynamic mode are shared across these views on the same server. Additional searches stay in the current view. Changing Spaces clears those local filters. Explicit filter links can open a different context.

Corpus · Read the evidence

Find the moments that matter

Saved sources, search text or #tags, and date rules determine which entries match. Select several Spaces to combine their matches without duplicates. Additional search filters narrow the combined activity.

Use Reset to return to the selected Space’s saved rules. To keep changed filters, choose Manage Spaces, select a destination, and save them there.

Kanban · Move work forward

Focus on relevant actions and ideas

The Space context follows you into the action board and ideas view. Link an action or idea to a Space in its editor to make that membership explicit, even when it does not match the Space’s saved entry filters.

Additional filters can still narrow what appears. Moving an action between status columns changes its workflow status; edit its Space assignment separately.

Graph · See the connections

Explore the same context visually

The Activity Graph uses the Space context to scope activity, linked objects, and their connections. Space-linked boards are included through their assignments.

Opening a board keeps its members together. Members outside the current Space filters are marked Outside Space filters so you can see the complete board without mistaking every member for a match.

Example · A Work Space

Read, act, then connect

Save work-related sources and tags. In Corpus, review matching entries. In Kanban, manage related actions and ideas. In Graph, explore their relationships and linked boards.

Use the same dropdown in each view for Dynamic mode, Visualize, Sharing preview, or Generate share recipe.

Filters and membership do different jobs

Saved filters find matching entries. Space assignments connect objects deliberately. Changing a filter does not reassign objects, and moving an action’s status does not move it to another Space.

Private until you choose to share

Saving a Space or linking a Facet does not publish anything.

A few useful details

Dates and schedules. Rolling dates move with today; fixed dates stay fixed. Schedules use your Profile timezone. An end before the start continues into the next day.

Removing a Space. Removes its saved filters and schedule, not your entries or other objects. Review linked Facets and object assignments afterward.
