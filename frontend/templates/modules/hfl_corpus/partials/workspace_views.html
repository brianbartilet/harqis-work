Combine and schedule views

Use Focus on to combine activity without duplicates. In Organize → Spaces, add weekly time blocks. Dynamic mode follows those blocks; gaps show all entries. Manual selection turns it off.

Example: the Eisenhower matrix

Group activity by urgency and importance using the Eisenhower matrix. Habit 3, “Put first things first,” in The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People uses the same four quadrants:

Q1 · Urgent and important

Deadlines and incidents

Q2 · Important, not urgent

Planning, learning, relationships

Q3 · Urgent, not important

Interruptions and distractions

Q4 · Neither urgent nor important

Low-value activities

Use these quadrants as examples for organizing your views. Entries are not classified automatically.

Learn more on Wikipedia: Eisenhower matrix · The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People.
