Share with intention

How share recipes work

Turn selected activity into a conversation.

A recipe describes what you want to share and how someone may access it. A generated share is the published, read-only experience reached through its link or QR code.

Saving is not publishing

Save recipe keeps reusable settings. Generate snapshot and QR creates a share after you confirm its recipient-visible name. A recipe name or recipient label alone does not restrict who can open a link.

01 · Scope

Choose the evidence

Start here or use Generate share recipe from Corpus, Kanban, or Graph. A Space handoff opens an unsaved draft with eligible context.

In Settings, choose activity sources, tags, dates, and a topic. Inherited Space rules still apply unless you explicitly replace them with custom filters. Search text from the activity page is not transferred.

02 · Review

Check every kind of content

Use Entries, Actions, Boards, Plans, and Graph to review the preview and exclude individual items. Entries are required; other sections can be disabled.

Explicitly Space-linked objects can qualify independently of entry source/date filters. Boards use Space membership; mapped goals follow selected boards. Review outside-Space board members and reference permissions separately.

03 · Personalize

Give the agent useful context

Open Agent → Personal context · Facets and soul. Select Soul sections, attachment excerpts, or approved journal observations. Linked Spaces suggest context without selecting it automatically.

Use the Markdown share context field for your purpose, audience, background, and preferred approach. The examples icon offers starting points for different conversations. Guidance shapes answers; it is not evidence for factual claims. Use Test agent to try a question against the current preview.

04 · Publish

Control the handoff

Review access mode, expiry, session limits, and permitted references. Generate the share, confirm its visible name, then give the intended recipient the QR code or link plus any required access instructions.

The QR and link lead to the same access flow. In generated-PIN mode, keep the PIN when shown—it is only revealed once.

Frozen or live: choose how context changes

Frozen: Freeze generated shares at creation keeps the reviewed snapshot. Later source edits do not silently replace it.

Live: turning Freeze off permits activity refresh. Review Automatically include for each content type: checked types can add newly matching objects; unchecked types refresh approved objects only. Exclusions still apply.

Personal context: selected Facet excerpts retain their chosen revisions, even with live activity. Facet edits and new journal approvals are not automatically published. Review updated selections in the recipe and explicitly update or generate the intended share.

What the recipient sees—and what chat knows

The published page provides chat and read-only views of the included activity. Additional sections appear when enabled and populated. Selected Facet excerpts appear under About the sharer; they are visible evidence, not hidden instructions.

The chat agent is instructed to answer from the supplied shared evidence, cite its sources, and say when context is insufficient. It cannot browse your private Facet library or unrelated activity, approve journal notes, or edit your records. Background and opportunity guidance cannot expand the shared scope.

References and original-file downloads need explicit permission. Including an extracted excerpt does not automatically allow the recipient to download its entire source file. Review the actual preview before publishing.

Access controls in plain language

  • Verification mode chooses the access check: QR possession, PIN, supported email OTP, or manual approval.
  • Expiry ends the share grant. Individual browser sessions also have their own lifetime.
  • Concurrent sessions limits simultaneous active sessions.
  • Maximum redemptions limits new sessions per cookie-identified browser, not verified people or total scans. Resuming an active session does not spend another redemption.
  • First successful session only makes the share single-use for redemption. Review this separately from concurrent-session limits.
Preview the content, not just its label

Check exclusions, references, personal excerpts, and live-inclusion settings. Leave secrets excluded unless deliberately needed and unlocked. Guidance may be reflected in answers; include only what the recipient should learn.

Recipes, personalization, and generated shares

Saved recipes lets you reload reusable settings. Editing a recipe is separate from updating an existing published share.

Share with builds personalized context from selected entities and optional Spaces. Review the resulting draft and its defaults before publishing; an entity or recipient label does not authenticate a person.

Generated shares is where you preview the published experience, reopen an available QR/link handoff, update eligible shares, or revoke access. Revoking is the way to stop a share; archiving a Facet does not revoke it.

Instant QR is a separate throwaway handoff from a saved recipe: QR access, one-day expiry, one concurrent session, and first successful session only. It allows selected references subject to exclusions and does not appear in Generated shares. Use normal generation when you need a managed share.
